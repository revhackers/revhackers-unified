## Stack Recomendado

| Budget | Stack |
|---|---|
| Lean (< R$500/mês) | Funnels + Apollo + Calendly + Loom |
| Growth (R$500-2k/mês) | Funnels + SalesNav + Aircall + Gong |
| Enterprise (> R$2k/mês) | Funnels + ZoomInfo + Outreach + Gong |
